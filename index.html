How grazing would work

Modelled, not live.
1 · Stake
USDC joins a shared pool. You hold a pro-rata claim, tracked by share — not a promised rate.
2 · It works
The pool fronts settlement so ramps clear instantly instead of waiting on bank rails.
3 · Fees split
A defined slice of each ramp fee goes to the pool, divided by share.
Why it isn't switched on. Taking deposits and paying returns is a regulated activity nearly everywhere. Get securities advice before this holds one real dollar.

How paying works

Amount first, QR second — and there's a reason for the order.
1 · Ask for the amount
Just the bill total. Don't ask for the QR yet.
2 · Place the order
This locks your exchange rate and reserves the fiat before you commit. If rates moved between scanning and paying, you'd get a nasty surprise — this removes that.
3 · Now scan
Point at the vendor's existing QR. They get local currency in their bank and never touch crypto.
